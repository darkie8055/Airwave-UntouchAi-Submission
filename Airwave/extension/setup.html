remote control.

Your hand is all you need. Give Airwave access to your webcam, then head back to YouTube to try your first gesture.

Chrome will ask for camera access. No microphone is requested.

◈

Your camera stays yours.

Hand tracking runs entirely on your laptop. No frames are recorded, saved, or sent to a server. This page tests access and immediately turns the camera off.

  1. Click Allow camera access and accept Chrome's prompt.
  2. Return to your YouTube tab and click the Airwave extension icon.
  3. Click Start camera, then hold an open palm to play.

If access is blocked, open Chrome Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Camera, remove Airwave's blocked entry, and retry. Also check your operating system's camera permissions.
